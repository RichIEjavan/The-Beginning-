## 1. Introduction
The Elderly Home Management System (EHMS) is a web-based application designed to help elderly care facilities manage residents, staff, appointments, medical records, and billing efficiently. This manual guides users through the system’s features and operations.

---

## 2. Getting Started
### 2.1 System Requirements
- **Server**: XAMPP (Apache, MySQL) installed locally.
- **Browser**: Google Chrome, Mozilla Firefox, or Microsoft Edge.
- **URL**: `http://localhost/elderly_home`

### 2.2 Accessing the System
1. Start XAMPP and ensure Apache and MySQL are running.
2. Open a web browser and navigate to `http://localhost/elderly_home`.
3. You will see the login page.

---

## 3. User Roles
The system supports four user roles with different permissions:

| Role         | Permissions                                                                 |
|--------------|-----------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **Admin**    | Full access: manage residents, staff, appointments, medical records, billing, and system settings. |
| **Doctor**   | View residents, manage appointments, add/view medical records.              |
| **Nurse**    | View residents, add observations to medical records.                        |
| **Receptionist** | Manage residents, schedule appointments, handle billing.                 |

---

## 4. Login and Dashboard
### 4.1 Login
1. Enter your **Username** and **Password**.
2. Click **Login**.
3. If credentials are correct, you will be redirected to your role-specific dashboard.

### 4.2 Dashboard
After login, you will see a dashboard with:
- **Statistics cards**: Number of active residents, today’s appointments, pending bills, etc.
- **Quick actions**: Buttons to add resident, schedule appointment, etc.
- **Upcoming appointments list** (if applicable to your role).

## 5. Managing Residents
### 5.1 Viewing Residents
- Click **Residents** in the navigation menu.
- A list of all residents appears, showing Name, DOB, Room, Status, and Actions.

### 5.2 Adding a New Resident
1. Click **Add New Resident** button.
2. Fill in the required fields (marked with *):
   - First Name*
   - Last Name*
   - Date of Birth*
   - Gender*
   - Emergency Contact Name
   - Emergency Contact Phone
   - Admission Date*
   - Room Number
3. Click **Save**. The new resident appears in the list.

### 5.3 Editing a Resident
- Click **Edit** next to the resident.
- Modify fields and click **Update**.

### 5.4 Discharging a Resident
- Click **Discharge** next to an active resident.
- Confirm the action; resident status changes to “Discharged”.

---

## 6. Managing Staff (Admin Only)
### 6.1 Viewing Staff
- Click **Staff** in the navigation.
- List of all staff members is displayed.

### 6.2 Adding New Staff
1. Click **Add New Staff**.
2. Enter:
   - First Name*
   - Last Name*
   - Role* (Doctor/Nurse/Receptionist/Admin)
   - Specialization
   - Phone
   - Email*
3. Click **Save**. A user account is automatically created with a default password (to be changed on first login).

### 6.3 Editing or Deleting Staff
- Use **Edit** or **Delete** buttons next to each staff record.

---

## 7. Appointments
### 7.1 Viewing Appointments
- Click **Appointments** in the menu.
- All appointments are listed with date, time, resident, doctor, reason, and status.

### 7.2 Scheduling an Appointment
1. Click **Schedule New Appointment**.
2. Select **Resident** and **Doctor** from dropdowns.
3. Choose **Date** and **Time**.
4. Enter **Reason** (optional).
5. Click **Schedule**.

### 7.3 Appointment Status
- **Scheduled**: Default when created.
- **Completed**: After check-out.
- **Cancelled**: If cancelled.

### 7.4 Check-In / Check-Out
- Receptionist can click **Check In** to record arrival time.
- After visit, click **Check Out** to mark as completed.

---

## 8. Medical Records
### 8.1 Viewing Medical Records
1. Go to **Residents**.
2. Click **Medical Records** next to a resident.
3. All records are displayed chronologically.

### 8.2 Adding a Medical Record
1. From the resident’s medical records page, click **Add New Record**.
2. Fill in:
   - Record Date*
   - Diagnosis
   - Observations
   - Prescriptions
3. Click **Save**.

---

## 9. Billing
### 9.1 Viewing Invoices
- Click **Billing** in the menu.
- List of all invoices with resident, description, amount, status, and due date.

### 9.2 Creating an Invoice
1. Click **Create New Invoice**.
2. Select **Resident**.
3. Enter **Description** (e.g., “Monthly Care Fee”).
4. Enter **Amount** (numeric).
5. Set **Due Date** (optional).
6. Click **Create**.

### 9.3 Marking an Invoice as Paid
- Click **Mark Paid** next to a pending invoice.
- Confirm; status changes to “Paid”.

---

## 10. Search
- Use the **Search** box in the top navigation bar.
- Enter a name (or partial name) or room number.
- Results show matching residents and staff.

## 11. Troubleshooting

| Problem                     | Possible Cause                          | Solution                                         |
|-----------------------------|-----------------------------------------|--------------------------------------------------|
| Cannot log in               | Incorrect username/password             | Verify credentials; contact admin if forgotten. |
| Page not loading            | Apache/MySQL not running                | Start XAMPP services.                             |
| Data not saving             | Required fields missing                 | Check for asterisks (*) and fill them.           |
| Access denied               | Insufficient permissions                | You are trying to access a page not allowed for your role. |
| System slow                 | Large data or server load               | Refresh page; if persists, contact IT.           |
